# Functional cases: CRM

Customers, leads, and follow-ups in the current .NET and React product. Case IDs in the table are execution IDs. They are not the requirement IDs in `design/`.

## What this product implements

| Area | UI | API client | Core |
|---|---|---|---|
| Customers | `frontend/react/src/pages/CrmPage.tsx` at `/crm` | `frontend/react/src/api/customers.ts` | `backend/dotnet/DealerOps.Core/Customer/` |
| Leads and follow-ups | `frontend/react/src/pages/LeadsPage.tsx` at `/leads` | `frontend/react/src/api/leads.ts` | `backend/dotnet/DealerOps.Core/Lead/` |
| Lead funnel | Lead section of `frontend/react/src/pages/SalesFunnelPage.tsx` at `/reports/sales-funnel`; overdue count on `frontend/react/src/pages/DashboardPage.tsx` | `frontend/react/src/api/reports.ts` (`/api/v1/leads/report/funnel`) | `LeadService.Funnel` |
| Buyer inquiry into CRM | `frontend/react/src/pages/ShopPage.tsx`; inbox **Open lead** in `frontend/react/src/components/JoinRequestInbox.tsx` | public inquiry, then the lead APIs above | `backend/dotnet/DealerOps.Core/BuyerSite/BuyerSiteService.cs` |

The browser calls only the gateway (`/api/v1/**`). Contract: `design/14-Backend-API-Contract.md` §5–§6 and `dealer-platform/openapi.yaml`. Routes: `frontend/react/src/router.tsx` (`DealerGuard` for `/crm`, `/leads`, and `/reports/sales-funnel`).

Vehicle-sales totals and workshop settlement on the sales report page are outside this CRM set.

## Out of the current product

Service tickets are not implemented. There is no ticket route, page, or API. `design/14-Backend-API-Contract.md` lists tickets as out of scope, and `design/13-Frontend-Engineering.md` says not to build a tickets page. No ticket cases are written here.
