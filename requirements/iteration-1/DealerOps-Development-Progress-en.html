1Project overview

Dealer Ops is a multi-tenant dealership operations platform. A single platform administrator provisions dealerships and creates employee accounts. Each dealership may have multiple accounts, and all employees in that dealership share the same dealership data.

The system has three connected business modules: vehicle management (DMS), customer relationship management (CRM), and advertising compliance. They are three front-end entry points over one backend and one database, not three independent applications. Compliance checks read vehicle and dealership data from the DMS and do not require duplicate entry.

2Scope

In scope

  • Multi-tenancy with dealership-level data isolation.
  • DMS vehicle fields; dealership-scoped unique VIN; paired sold date and sold price; purchase fields locked after sale.
  • CRM name, email, phone and address, with dealership vehicle associations.
  • Advertising compliance using deterministic rules plus real AI review; Ready marking and TXT export.
  • Audit records for every DMS and CRM change.
  • Read-only AI queries over the current dealership.
  • Three sign-in paths — local password using email, username or phone; Google ID token; and Firebase Auth — plus self-registration; roles are Platform.Admin and Dealer.User. A new account from any path starts unbound, and the Firebase path accepts only verified emails and refuses platform administrators — see section 7a.
  • Public landing page with a VIN decoder, and the five client-note extensions, all documented in design/21-Feature-Extensions.md: VIN-01–04, LEAD-01–09, WO-01–07, AUTH-16–23 and IMG-01–05. See section 7a for their status.
  • Gateway-only browser access to backend APIs.

Explicitly out of scope

  • Sales-funnel reports, vehicle archive state and garage/repair billing.
  • Buyer website, public inventory page and inquiry form.
  • KPI dashboard and CSV import.
  • Automatic publishing to third-party advertising platforms, payments and contracts.
  • OCR, mileage, color, fuel, stock number and list-price fields outside the specification.
  • Message queues, a second database and a vector database.
  • Rate-limit quotas, throughput SLOs and PIPEDA retention policy.

Class demonstration path: two dealerships with one employee each remain isolated; administrator access to vehicle APIs is denied; missing price or finance APR is blocked; a compliant advertisement is reviewed by the real model; editing a vehicle or advertisement invalidates the old result.

4.2 State model

[new] → IN_STOCK → (sold date + sold price entered together) → SOLD
  • Only IN_STOCK and SOLD exist; there is no reconditioning, pending-sale or archive state.
  • The server derives state from the two sold fields.
  • Sale reversal / return is out of scope.

6Advertising compliance

Before an advertisement is marked ready, the platform checks OMVIC-oriented disclosure requirements. BLOCK stops Ready and TXT export; REVIEW produces a warning only.

Delivery boundary: “Publish” means mark Ready and export TXT. There is no external advertising integration or buyer website. Checks read title/body, DMS vehicle data and public dealership information; no new vehicle fields or sales-staff checkboxes are added.

6.1 Inputs

  • One draft per dealership vehicle.
  • Title and body are editable; an empty draft may be saved but cannot pass.
  • Types: CASH, FINANCE, LEASE. Media: ONLINE or RADIO_TV_BILLBOARD.

6.2 Required disclosure rules

ScopeDisclosure
All adsRegistered dealership name and contact details; prior use when applicable; new/used status and year; warranty terms when offered; price; condition.
Finance adsAPR, term, and cash price or cost of borrowing. Radio/TV/billboard is exempt from the online proximity check, not from price or APR.
Lease adsLease statement, term, monthly payment, APR, down payment and excess-kilometre fee when the annual allowance is below 20,000 km.

6.3 Rule catalogue

IDCheckResult / code
AD-R01Registered dealership name missingBLOCK DEALER_NAME_MISSING

AD-R20–R24Lease statement, term, monthly payment, APR and down paymentLease statement and APR: BLOCK; term, payment and down payment: REVIEW
AD-R25Annual kilometre allowance and excess-km feeAllowance stated below 20,000 km/year with no per-km excess cost: BLOCK LEASE_EXCESS_KM_MISSING. Exactly 20,000 or more: no requirement. Allowance unstated: REVIEW LEASE_ALLOWANCE_UNSTATED

A finance rule is triggered by type FINANCE, or by a CASH ad containing a rate (APR or not) or payment patterns such as monthly, biweekly or weekly payments. Lease uses its own rules.

  • FINANCE_CASH_PRICE_MISSING is a BLOCK that the generic price rule does not cover: an amount directly followed by a cadence (per month, /mo, /month, a month, bi-weekly, weekly) is a payment, not a cash price, so finance copy whose only figure is "$299/month" is blocked on every medium.
  • FINANCE_APR_PROXIMITY checks "shown as prominently as the payment" as placement (changed 2026-10-20): it is raised when an online finance ad shows an APR and a payment amount but prints no APR within 80 characters of a payment. An APR next to the payment passes; it never blocks.
  • Tightened 2026-10-20: a rate without the word APR ("0% financing") triggers the finance rules; "$2,000 down" is not a cash price; "12 month warranty" is not a finance term; the lease down payment needs its amount and the excess-km charge needs its per-km cost.
  • LEASE_RENT_MISSING was corrected on 2026-10-16. Its predicate also required a missing price, which a PRICE_MISSING block already covered, so the rule could never fire on its own and the specification's lease payment-amount disclosure went unchecked. It now tests the payment cadence alone and stays REVIEW under Correction 4.

6.4 Check lifecycle

Save draft → Check ├─ any BLOCK rule → BLOCKED (AI skipped, HTTP 200) └─ no BLOCK → real AI review (15-second timeout) ├─ success → PASSED (REVIEW warnings and AI opinions retained) └─ timeout/error → AI_UNAVAILABLE (public API 502; never treated as passed) Before checking: NEEDS_AI Edit ad or vehicle condition after pass: STALE Edit a failed ad: NEEDS_AI PASSED with matching content version: Ready and TXT export allowed
  • PASSED means deterministic hard checks passed and AI responded; it is not legal approval.
  • AI opinions do not veto Ready.
  • Ready/export require PASSED and a matching content version, otherwise NOT_PASSED or CHECK_STALE.
  • Changes to title, body, type, media, price text or vehicle condition invalidate a result.

8Cross-cutting requirements

8.1 Audit

  • Vehicle create/edit/sale and customer create/edit/link/unlink are audited.
  • Each event contains actor username, action, UTC timestamp, object type and object ID. The action set is CREATE, UPDATE, SELL, LINK, UNLINK, plus NOTE for lead notes and DELETE for account self-deletion and vehicle-photo removal.
  • Customer phone/email/address values are never stored in audit summaries.
  • A change records the field name and its new value, not a before/after snapshot. The specification asks only for actor, action and timestamp, so the fuller snapshot described in earlier analysis drafts is not a requirement.
  • Audit is append-only and business writes share a transaction with their audit event.
  • Vehicle and customer detail pages show newest history first; vehicle LINK/UNLINK display remains a documented partial gap.

9.2 UI requirements

  • Unauthenticated access redirects to /login and preserves the requested destination.
  • Admin lands on /admin; bound users land on /dms; unbound users see a no-access shell, which a guard renders in place of the page rather than at its own route.
  • The read-only assistant is a floating panel shown only to bound Dealer.User accounts on business pages, not a route; administrators and unbound accounts never see it. It is hidden on /, /login and /register. There is no /assistant path.
  • Vehicle and customer detail are drawers on the list page, not separate routes; there is no /dms/vehicles/:id path. Deep links use query strings such as /dms?vehicleId=, /crm?customerId=, /ads?vehicleId= and /leads?leadId=.
  • An unknown path redirects to /; there is no 404 page.
  • Lists default to ten rows and provide loading, empty, error and retry states.
  • Forms show field-level validation and preserve the server error code/message.
  • Vehicle and customer details provide cross-navigation and newest-first audit history.
  • Advertising checklists are informational only; the UI never collects checkbox answers.
  • AI pages clearly distinguish deterministic BLOCK/REVIEW results from non-blocking AI opinions.
  • Supported assistant counts use all matching tenant records, independently of the five-card preview limit (UI-52).

11.2 Adopted assumptions

  • Vehicle source has four fixed values: trade-in, auction, private purchase and other.
  • Year range is 1900 through current year +1; VIN is standard 17-character format.
  • Purchased vehicles are optional until a purchase occurs.
  • CRM association and DMS sale are separate operations.
  • Vehicle/customer deletion and sale reversal are not supported.
  • Price and new/used status are checked in ad copy, not added to DMS.
  • Prior use and warranty are inferred only when copy contains clues; absent copy cannot be inferred.
  • Exactly 20,000 km/year does not require an excess-km fee because the source says “below 20,000”.
  • Finance rules trigger by type or by APR/payment patterns in a cash ad.
  • One vehicle has one advertisement; audit records modifications, not reads.
  • There is one platform administrator initialized from environment variables.
  • Rules are hard-coded in OmvicRuleEngine for this version.
