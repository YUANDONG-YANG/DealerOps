## CR-063

**Statement:** The sample ads on the home page should link to sign-up instead of sign-in.

**Status:** implemented

**Place named:** Home page (`/`, `LandingPage` in `frontend/react/src/pages/LandingPage.tsx`). `frontend/react/src/router.tsx` maps `/` to `LandingPage` and `/register` to `RegisterPage`.

**Present:**

- The three sample ads are `PREVIEW_ROWS`. The preview head reads “Sample ad checks · tap a car.”
- Each row is a `button` (`landing-preview-row`) whose click calls `setOpenSample`. That click stays on the home page and opens the sample check (`landing-sample`).
- Inside the open check, the only navigation control is `Link` to `/register` with the text “Check your own ads →”.
- `RegisterPage` (`frontend/react/src/pages/RegisterPage.tsx`) is the sign-up screen: heading “Create account”, submit “Create account”, and `register` from `frontend/react/src/auth/login.ts`.
- Header, hero, and closing “Sign in” buttons on `LandingPage` still use `Link` to `/login`. Those buttons are separate from the sample ads. The decorative `CTA_ADS` conveyor is `aria-hidden` and has no link.

**Missing:** Nothing the statement requires. The sample row itself is not a route link; the sign-up link is the control inside the opened sample. No sample-ad control still points at sign-in.

## CR-064

**Statement:** The home page should say more prominently that it is for dealerships and not for customers.

**Status:** implemented

**Place named:** Home page (`LandingPage`). The source does not supply the sentence.

**Present:**

- Directly under the landing nav, `LandingPage` renders `div.landing-audience` with `role="note"`. The strong text is “Dealer Ops is software for car dealerships, not a place to buy a car.” The next sentence sends vehicle shoppers to `/shop` (“Shop vehicles from our dealerships”).
- `.landing-audience` in `frontend/react/src/styles.css` is a bordered callout (left bar, soft background, 15px) placed above the hero. The hero still has the eyebrow “Dealership back office” and the heading “Run your dealership with ads that are checked before they go out.” The closing call to action says “Dealership staff sign in with the account their dealership or platform administrator set up.”

**Missing:** Nothing the statement requires. The source does not define the wording or a font size. The audience sentence is the banner above the hero, not a second page title.

## CR-065

**Statement:** The dealership shop page should not say "used," because dealerships also hold and sell new vehicles.

**Status:** implemented

**Place named:** Home page > shop vehicles (`/shop`, `DealerDirectory` in `frontend/react/src/pages/ShopPage.tsx`). `router.tsx` maps `/shop` and `/shop/:dealer` to `ShopPage`. The analysis treats this as page copy. It does not add a new-versus-used column. `condition_code` stays a disclosure code.

**Present:**

- `DealerDirectory` heading is “Shop vehicles.” The line under it is “Choose a dealership to see the vehicles it has listed.”
- `DealerInventory` (the dealership page at `/shop/:dealer`) uses the dealership `legalName` as its heading. It does not add a “used” title.
- A listed vehicle’s facts include `enumLabel(v.conditionCode)` (`frontend/react/src/format.ts`). `ConditionCode` (`backend/dotnet/DealerOps.Core/Vehicle/ConditionCode.cs`) is `Certified`, `AsIs`, `Unfit`, and `Irreparable`. None of those values is a new/used flag.
- `BuyerSiteService.Inventory` (`backend/dotnet/DealerOps.Core/BuyerSite/BuyerSiteService.cs`) fills `PublicVehicle` (`backend/dotnet/DealerOps.Core/BuyerSite/Dto/PublicInventory.cs`) with the listing `Title` and `Body` the dealership already stored. It does not prefix those strings with “used.” A search of `backend/dotnet` found no “used vehicles” or “Shop used” string.

**Missing:** Nothing the statement requires on the shop page’s own labels. A dealership can still type “Used” into an ad title or body, and `DealerInventory` renders that text through `AdText`. The three home-page sample ads in `PREVIEW_ROWS` still begin with “Used”; those strings are on `/`, not on the shop page this item names.
