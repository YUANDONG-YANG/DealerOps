## CR-045

**Statement:** Creating a customer whose name, email, phone, or address matches an existing customer should ask for confirmation first.

**Status:** implemented

**Place named:** Any user account > CRM > Add customer + Edit. The same paragraph also covers customer creation through the leads page. The sample notice names the matching field and asks whether to create the customer anyway. The source does not say what to do when more than one field matches.

**Present:**

- `CustomerService.Duplicates` (`backend/dotnet/DealerOps.Core/Customer/CustomerService.cs`) returns `name`, `email`, `phone`, and `homeAddress`, in that order, when another customer of the dealership matches. Name and address are trimmed exact matches. Email uses `NormalizeEmail` (trim and lower case). Phone is compared on digits only. `excludeId` skips the customer being edited. `CustomerController.Duplicates` exposes this as `GET /api/v1/customers/duplicates` and returns `CustomerDuplicatesResponse`. The gateway route `dealer-core-public` in `backend/dotnet/DealerOps.Gateway/appsettings.json` forwards `/api/v1/{**catch-all}` to Core, so the browser path is the gateway.
- `customersApi.duplicates` and `duplicateCustomerMessage` (`frontend/react/src/api/customers.ts`) build “A customer with this {fields} already exists. Create anyway?” (or “Save anyway?”). Several matches are joined (“name and phone”).
- CRM: `CrmPage.save` calls that lookup before `persist`. A match opens `ConfirmDialog` titled “Possible duplicate customer” (`confirmLabel` “Create anyway” or “Save anyway”). Cancel clears the message and does not save. Confirm calls `confirmDuplicate`, which calls `persist`. This runs for add and for edit (`selected` is passed as `excludeId`).
- Leads: `LeadsPage.create`, when `customerMode === 'new'`, runs the same lookup before `submitCreate`. The dialog uses “Create anyway”. `submitCreate` posts `newCustomer` through `leadsApi.create`. `LeadService.Create` (`backend/dotnet/DealerOps.Core/Lead/LeadService.cs`) creates that customer with `CustomerService.Create`.
- A failed duplicate lookup sets an error and does not save (`CrmPage.save`, `LeadsPage.create`).

**Missing:** Nothing the statement requires. `CustomerService.Create` and `Patch` do not take a confirm flag; a caller that skips the lookup can still save. The pages above do not skip it. A duplicate email still returns `emailWarning` from `DuplicateEmailWarning` after the confirmed save (the sentence “Another customer at this dealership already uses this email. The customer was saved.”). That warning is in addition to the confirm.

## CR-046

**Statement:** Customer phone numbers should not be unique, because people may share a phone and still need separate customer profiles.

**Status:** implemented

**Place named:** Same paragraph as CR-045: regular customer creation, customer creation through the leads page, and CRM > Add customer + Edit.

**Present:**

- `CustomerService.Create` and `Patch` store the trimmed phone and do not reject a number another customer already has. The create comment states that no customer field is unique. There is no `RequireUniquePhone` and no customer `PHONE_TAKEN` in `DealerOps.Core`.
- `CustomerEntity` (`backend/dotnet/DealerOps.Core/Customer/CustomerEntity.cs`) maps `phone` with a max length and no unique index. `AppDbContext.ConfigureCustomer` unique-indexes the active vehicle link only (`uk_cv_active_vehicle`). The startup `ALTER TABLE` list in `backend/dotnet/DealerOps.Core/Program.cs` adds `customer.notes` and `customer.deleted_at` and does not add a unique key on `customer.phone`.
- Lead creation of a new customer uses that same `CustomerService.Create`, so a shared phone is accepted there too. CRM edit uses `Patch`.
- Account phones are a separate rule and stay unique: `AppDbContext` index `uk_app_user_phone` on `app_user`, and `NewAccountRules` maps a duplicate to `PHONE_TAKEN`. The change note does not lift that rule (`08-api-and-integrations.md` §5.3).

**Missing:** Nothing the statement requires for customer profiles. `BuyerSiteService.Inquire` (`backend/dotnet/DealerOps.Core/BuyerSite/BuyerSiteService.cs`) still attaches a public inquiry to the first active customer whose stored phone string equals the typed phone, and its comment still says the phone is unique per dealership. The change document does not mention that path. It does not reject a second CRM customer with the same phone.

## CR-047

**Statement:** A VIN on the DMS page must be forced to match the vehicle information.

**Status:** partial

**Place named:** DMS page. The source does not say which vehicle fields must match, or whether Decode is the check (`07-workflows-and-ui.md` W19, `09-gaps-risks-ambiguities.md` A-11).

**Present:**

- `VehicleService.RequireVinMatchesModelYear` (`backend/dotnet/DealerOps.Core/Vehicle/VehicleService.cs`) runs on `Create`, and on `Patch` when `vin` or `modelYear` changed. For model year 1980 or later and a 17-character VIN, character 10 must be the model-year code for that year. The code repeats every 30 years, so one character accepts either year of the pair. A mismatch throws `400 VALIDATION` with `fieldErrors.vin` (“VIN does not match the model year…” or “is not a model-year code”). Years before 1980 skip the check. A VIN shorter or longer than 17 also skips this method; format is a separate check.
- `vinYearError` in `frontend/react/src/vehicleRules.ts` repeats that year-code rule. `DmsPage.validateForm` sets `e.vin` from it when the VIN or year differs from the stored vehicle. `DmsPage.save` returns without calling create or update when `validateForm` has errors.
- Sold vehicles cannot change make, model, year, or VIN: `PurchaseFieldsChanged` plus the sold lock in `VehicleService.Patch`. `validateForm` returns early for a sold vehicle and does not re-check the year code.

**Missing:** Make, model, body type, and the other decoded facts are not compared to the VIN. `DmsPage.decodeVin` calls `catalogApi.decodeVin`, which hits `VehicleCatalogService.DecodeVinAsync` (`backend/dotnet/DealerOps.Core/Catalog/VehicleCatalogService.cs`). Decode fills make, model, year, and body on the form and does not refuse a later edit of those fields. A user can save a VIN whose year code matches the entered year and whose make and model do not match a decode. The statement asks for a match to “vehicle information” and does not name the year code as the only field.
