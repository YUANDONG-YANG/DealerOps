1Project overview

Dealer Ops is a multi-tenant dealership management platform. One platform admin opens dealerships and issues logins to dealership staff. A dealership can have several logins (one per staff member), and all of them share that dealership's data.

The system has three related business modules: vehicle management (DMS), customer relationship management (CRM) and ad compliance. They are three front-end entry points on one back end and one database, not three separate applications. Ad checks read DMS vehicle data and the dealer profile directly, so nothing is entered twice.

2Scope

In scope for this delivery

  • Multi-tenancy: data is isolated between dealerships, and the admin sees no business data
  • DMS: the vehicle fields in the specification; VIN unique per dealership; sold date and price entered as a pair; purchase fields locked after sale
  • CRM: name, email, phone, home address; links to this dealership's vehicles; one customer per vehicle
  • Ad compliance check: fixed rule list + real AI review; once passed, the ad can be marked Ready and exported as TXT
  • Audit: every DMS/CRM change records the actor, the action and the time
  • Read-only AI assistant: queries this dealership's data and never writes to the database
  • Sign-in: local passwords with email, username or phone; Google ID tokens and Firebase Auth are also supported. Every new account is an unbound Dealer.User, and the only roles are Platform.Admin / Dealer.User
  • Feature extensions: VIN decode, lead follow-up, vehicle reconditioning work orders, Image Studio (as set out in the section 12 change log and design/21-Feature-Extensions.md)
  • Gateway: the browser reaches the back end only through the Gateway

Explicitly out of scope

  • Sales funnel reports, vehicle archive states, garage/service invoicing
  • Buyer website, public inventory page, inquiry form
  • KPI dashboard, CSV import
  • Automatic publishing to third-party ad platforms, payments, contracts
  • OCR, automatic publishing to third-party ad platforms, payments and contracts
  • Fields outside the specification, such as mileage, colour, fuel, stock number and list price
  • Message queues, a second database, vector stores
  • Rate-limit quotas, throughput SLOs, PIPEDA retention policy

Classroom demo storyline: two dealerships, each with one staff member, isolated from each other; the admin is refused on the vehicle API; an ad with a missing price, or a finance ad with no APR, is blocked; one real ad goes through a real model review; after a price change, the old check result can no longer be exported.

3.4 Account lifecycle

Admin creates dealership → Admin binds staff (username, email and/or phone + temporary password → dealership) → Staff sign in with email/username/phone → Use own dealership's data ↓ Admin unbinds → Staff call a business endpoint again → 403
  • After a login is removed, its audit records are kept and the "actor" is never lost.
  • After a dealership's last login is removed, the dealership's data is kept, and new logins can be bound later.
  • Re-binding an unbound user counts as reactivation: binding to the same dealership reuses the existing binding row and creates no new account; binding to a different dealership adds a new binding row; on re-binding the account keeps its original password (per the API contract, design/14 §3.6), and a forgotten password is reset separately by the admin.

4.2 Vehicle status

[New] ──► IN_STOCK in stock ──(sold date + sold price entered together)──► SOLD sold
  • There are only two statuses; there is no reconditioning, pending-sale or archived status.
  • The server derives the status: SOLD when both sold fields have values, otherwise IN_STOCK.
  • Undoing a sale (vehicle return) is not supported in this release.

4.4 Edge cases

  • Year = current year + 1 (an early-release model year) is accepted; + 2 is rejected.
  • A purchase cost of 0 (for example, a trade-in credit) is accepted.
  • A sold price below cost is allowed and not blocked.
  • An IRREPARABLE vehicle can be added and sold (sold for parts), but its ad must disclose the condition truthfully.

5.2 Link rules

  • Customer : vehicle = 1 : N (one customer can buy several vehicles).
  • Vehicle : customer = 1 : 0..1 (a vehicle belongs to at most one customer).
  • Only vehicles of the same dealership can be linked; any vehicle that is in stock or sold and not yet linked can be linked.
  • A sold vehicle's link cannot be removed (the sales record cannot be erased).

6Ad compliance check

Before an ad is published, the system checks its copy against the disclosure requirements of OMVIC (the Ontario Motor Vehicle Industry Council) and flags what is missing. Course delivery uses the simplified rules set out in scope baseline errata 3–4: BLOCK is a hard stop that prevents Ready and export; REVIEW is a hint that does not block.

Delivery boundary: "Publish" = mark Ready + export TXT; there is no integration with external platforms and no buyer page. The check reads only the title + body, the DMS vehicle data and the public dealer profile; no vehicle fields are added and no salesperson checklist answers are collected. Prior use or a warranty that the copy never mentions cannot be inferred; this is an accepted demo limitation.

6.1 Ad input

  • Pick a vehicle of this dealership; one ad per vehicle.
  • Title and body are editable text; an empty draft can be saved but cannot pass the check.
  • Ad kind: CASH cash / FINANCE finance / LEASE lease. Medium: ONLINE online / RADIO_TV_BILLBOARD radio, TV or billboard.

6.5 Check flow and states

Save draft → click "Check" ├─ Any BLOCK rule hit ──────────► BLOCKED (AI skipped, HTTP 200) └─ No BLOCK ──► Call the real AI (15 s timeout) ├─ Success ──────► PASSED (REVIEW hints and AI notes kept) └─ Timeout/error ──► AI_UNAVAILABLE (public API 502, never shown as passed) Before any check: NEEDS_AI Ad or condition changed after a pass ──► STALE Ad changed while not passed ──► back to NEEDS_AI PASSED and version matches ──► "Mark Ready" and "Export TXT" available
  • PASSED means the demo's hard rules passed and the AI responded normally. It does not mean legal compliance approval, and the result may still contain hints.
  • AI notes do not veto Ready.
  • Changes that trigger invalidation: the ad title, body, kind or medium (including a price change in the body), and the vehicle condition. Other vehicle or dealer profile changes do not trigger invalidation.
  • Ready / export require PASSED with a matching content version; otherwise they return NOT_PASSED / CHECK_STALE.
