## CR-001

**Statement:** The DMS page must gain Sold by, Sold to, and Date sold columns.

**Status:** implemented

**Place named:** DMS vehicle inventory (`/dms`, `DmsPage` in `frontend/react/src/pages/DmsPage.tsx`). The inventory placement note says a screenshot arrow marks the gap between Stock # and Body. The statement itself does not give an insert index. `09-gaps-risks-ambiguities.md` says that caption does not mark an insertion point.

**Present:**

- `DmsPage` builds `columns` with `soldBy` labeled “Sold by”, `soldToName` labeled “Sold to”, and `soldOn` labeled “Date sold”. Sold to renders `linkedCustomer.name`, then `soldToName`, then an em dash. Sold by and Date sold render the field or an em dash. The table is `DataTable` on that page.
- Those three columns sit after Leads and before Status. The earlier columns are Year Make Model, VIN, Stock #, Body, Source, Condition, Cost, List price, Date added, Open WOs, and Leads.
- `VehicleResponse` (`backend/dotnet/DealerOps.Core/Vehicle/Dto/VehicleResponse.cs`) includes `SoldOn`, `SoldBy`, `SoldToName`, and `LinkedCustomer`. `VehicleService.ToResponse` copies `vehicle.SoldOn`, `vehicle.SoldBy`, and `vehicle.SoldToName`, and loads the linked customer name.
- `VehicleService.List` returns that projection. `SortFields` includes `soldOn`, `soldBy`, and `soldToName`. Sold-to sort uses the linked customer name, or `SoldToName` when there is no link.
- `VehicleEntity` stores `SoldOn` (`sold_on`), `SoldBy` (`sold_by`, username of the member who recorded the sale), and `SoldToName` (`sold_to_name`, typed buyer when the sale is “Other”). `Program.cs` in `DealerOps.Core` adds `sold_by` and `sold_to_name` if the columns are missing.
- `VehicleService.Sell` sets `SoldOn` from the request, `SoldBy` from `TenantContext.ActorUsername()`, and `SoldToName` to the typed name only when there is no CRM buyer. A CRM buyer is linked, and the column prefers that customer’s name.
- `vehiclesApi.list` and `vehiclesApi.get` (`frontend/react/src/api/vehicles.ts`) call `GET /api/v1/vehicles` and `GET /api/v1/vehicles/{id}`. `DealerOps.Gateway` `Program.cs` forwards `/api/v1` with `MapReverseProxy`.

**Missing:** Nothing the statement requires. The three columns are not inserted between Stock # and Body; that position is only the inventory’s reading of the screenshot. Sold by is the username, which the change document does not distinguish from a display name.

## CR-002

**Statement:** The sell-vehicle menu must also show cost, the total spent on work orders, and list price.

**Status:** implemented

**Place named:** The sell action on DMS. The source sentence says “cost, total number spend on work orders, and list price.” The inventory reads that as a money total. `02-new-capabilities.md` leaves open whether the work-order phrase is a count or a sum, and whether it means `repairCost`. `08-api-and-integrations.md` says `repairCost` is also editable on create and patch, so it is not guaranteed to equal the sum of work-order costs.

**Present:**

- `DmsPage` opens one `ConfirmDialog` (`sellOpen`, title from `sellTitle`) from the row action `openSell` and from “Mark as sold” (`beginSell`). `openSell` loads the vehicle with `vehiclesApi.get` before `beginSell`.
- The dialog shows three read-only figures before sold date, sold price, and buyer: “Cost” is `cad(edit.purchaseCost)`, “Work-order spend” is `cad(edit.repairCost ?? 0)`, and “List price” is `cad(edit.listPrice)` or “Not set”.
- `VehicleResponse` carries `PurchaseCost`, `RepairCost`, and `ListPrice`. `VehicleService.Get` returns `ToResponse`.
- `WorkOrderService.Patch` (`backend/dotnet/DealerOps.Core/WorkOrder/WorkOrderService.cs`), when the order becomes done, calls `VehicleService.AddRepairCost`, which adds that cost onto `vehicle.RepairCost`. The add-vehicle and edit form in `DmsPage` also has a “Repair cost” field written through `vehiclesApi` as `repairCost`.

**Missing:** Nothing the statement requires if “total spent” is the stored repair total. The dialog does not sum work-order `cost` rows on its own, and it does not show a count of work orders. A repair cost typed on the vehicle form is included in the same “Work-order spend” figure.

## CR-003

**Statement:** The sales report must offer current day, week, month, and year as separate options.

**Status:** implemented

**Place named:** Sales report (`/reports/sales-funnel`, `SalesFunnelPage` in `frontend/react/src/pages/SalesFunnelPage.tsx`, registered in `frontend/react/src/router.tsx`). The document does not name the measures, the week start, or the timezone. `08-api-and-integrations.md` treats one selected period per request as inferred.

**Present:**

- `SALES_PERIODS` in `frontend/react/src/api/reports.ts` is Today (`TODAY`), This week (`WEEK`), This month (`MONTH`), and This year (`YEAR`). `SalesFunnelPage` keeps `period` in state, default `MONTH`, and renders a select labeled “Sales period” under the heading “Sales for {period}”.
- Changing the select calls `reportsApi.sales(period)`, which is `GET /api/v1/vehicles/report/sales` with query `period`.
- `VehicleController.SalesReport` (`backend/dotnet/DealerOps.Core/Vehicle/VehicleController.cs`) takes `[FromQuery] SalesPeriod? period` and calls `VehicleService.SalesReport`. `SnakeCaseEnumModelBinder` accepts the upper-snake query values (`TODAY`, `WEEK`, `MONTH`, `YEAR`). JSON enums use the same upper-snake form (`Program.cs` in `DealerOps.Core`).
- `VehicleService.SalesReport` keeps sales with status sold, a sold date, and a sold price. For a period it keeps rows whose `SoldOn` is from the period start through `Today()` inclusive. `Today()` is `DateOnly.FromDateTime(DateTime.UtcNow)`. Today starts on that UTC date. Week starts on Monday (`AddDays(-(((int)today.DayOfWeek + 6) % 7))`). Month starts on the first of the UTC month. Year starts on 1 January of the UTC year.
- The period block shows `sales.period.soldCount` (“Vehicles sold”), `revenue`, and `grossProfit` (“Sold price − purchase − repair”), with the `from` and `to` dates on the vehicles-sold card. The page still shows all-time totals and up to twelve month rows below that block. The statement does not remove those.

**Missing:** Nothing the statement requires. The four periods are one select, not four panels at once. “Current” is the UTC calendar, which the document does not define.

## CR-004

**Statement:** The sales section of the dashboard must show the same day, week, month, and year sales information.

**Status:** implemented

**Place named:** Dashboard Sales section (`/dashboard`, `DashboardPage` in `frontend/react/src/pages/DashboardPage.tsx`). The inventory illustration is a red arrow on that section. “The same” refers to the day, week, month, and year sales information in CR-003. The employee list is the next inventory item (CR-005) and is not part of this statement.

**Present:**

- `DashboardPage` uses the same `SALES_PERIODS` and the same `reportsApi.sales(period)` call. State defaults to `MONTH`. The select sits on the `h2` “Sales” and is labeled “Sales period”.
- That section shows “Vehicles sold” from `sales.period.soldCount` with the `from`–`to` hint, “Revenue” from `sales.period.revenue`, and “Gross profit” from `sales.period.grossProfit`. Those are the same period fields the sales report shows for the selected option.
- A fourth card, “Avg. days to sell”, uses `sales.averageDaysToSell` and is hinted “All time”. It is the lifetime figure from `VehicleService.SalesReport`, not the selected period.
- The same gateway proxy and `VehicleController.SalesReport` path serve this page. The route is behind `DealerGuard` in `router.tsx`.

**Missing:** Nothing the statement requires. The dashboard Sales section does not repeat the salesperson table or the all-time month table that also appear on the sales report. The average-days card stays all-time while the other three cards follow the selected period.
