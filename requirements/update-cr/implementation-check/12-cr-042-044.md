## CR-042

**Statement:** The CRM vehicles-purchased column should be replaced by the customer's leads.

**Status:** implemented

**Place named:** Logged in as any user account > CRM (`CrmPage` in `frontend/react/src/pages/CrmPage.tsx`). The source does not say whether the new column is a count, a list, or a link (`03-modified-behavior.md`; `09-gaps-risks-ambiguities.md` A-20). It does not ask to delete purchase links (`04-removals-and-deprecations.md`, `05-data-model.md` §4).

**Present:**

- `CrmPage` builds `columns` as Name, Email, Phone, Home address, Linked vehicles, and Leads. The Leads column is `{ key: 'leadCount', label: 'Leads' }`. No column label in that array is "Vehicles purchased".
- `DataTable` (`frontend/react/src/components/DataTable.tsx`) prints `row[col.key]` when a column has no `render`. The Leads cell is therefore the numeric `leadCount`, including `0`.
- Linked vehicles is a separate column. Its `render` shows `row.linkedVehicle` (year, make, model via `linkedLabel`) or "None", plus "+N more" from `row.linkedVehicleCount` when more than one vehicle is linked.
- `CustomerController.List` (`backend/dotnet/DealerOps.Core/Customer/CustomerController.cs`) returns `PageResponse<CustomerListItem>` from `GET /api/v1/customers`.
- `CustomerListItem` (`backend/dotnet/DealerOps.Core/Customer/Dto/CustomerListItem.cs`) has both `LinkedVehicleCount` and `LeadCount`.
- `CustomerService.ToListItem` (`backend/dotnet/DealerOps.Core/Customer/CustomerService.cs`) sets `leadCount` with `_db.Leads.Count(l => l.CustomerId == customer.Id)`. The count is every lead for that customer. The method comment says the list shows leads in place of the purchase count.
- The same page's customer drawer lists the lead rows, not only the count. `loadCustomerLeads` calls `leadsApi.list({ customerId, page, size: 10 })`. `leadsApi.list` (`frontend/react/src/api/leads.ts`) sends `customerId` on `GET /api/v1/leads`. `LeadController.List` passes that query to `LeadService.List`, which filters `l.CustomerId == customerId`. Each row links to `/leads?leadId=` and shows stage, vehicle, and follow-up.

**Missing:** Nothing the statement requires. The list cell is the count of leads in any stage, not the lead records; those records are in the customer drawer. Linked vehicles stays on the list and still uses `linkedVehicleCount` for the "+N more" hint. The statement replaces the vehicles-purchased column and does not say to remove linked-vehicle display.

## CR-043

**Statement:** A lead should be addable from inside the customer menu.

**Status:** implemented

**Place named:** The inventory does not name a route. W17 places this on the CRM customer drawer (`CrmPage`). The source does not name the control or the fields. W17 says the action should open the existing lead form, or go to `/leads`, rather than add a new route. `02-new-capabilities.md` §4.3 treats it as another entry to the existing create, with the open customer implied.

**Present:**

- When a customer is open (`selected`), the customer `FormDrawer` (title "Customer") renders a `Link` labeled "New lead" to `/leads?newLeadFor=${selected.id}`. That block is under the linked-vehicle section, next to the "Leads" heading. It is not on the create-customer form, which has no `selected`.
- `LeadsPage` (`frontend/react/src/pages/LeadsPage.tsx`) reads `newLeadFor`. The effect calls `customersApi.get(customerId)` and then `openCreate(r.data)`. The comment on that effect names this query as the CRM action.
- `openCreate` sets `customerMode` to `'existing'`, `createForm.customerId` to that customer's `id`, and `customerText` to the customer's name. The Add lead drawer (`FormDrawer` title "Add lead") shows the Existing customer search with that name.
- Save goes through `LeadsPage` `submitCreate` to `leadsApi.create`, which posts `{ customerId, ... }` to `POST /api/v1/leads`. `LeadController.Create` calls `LeadService.Create` (`backend/dotnet/DealerOps.Core/Lead/LeadService.cs`). When `CustomerId` is set, that method loads the customer in the caller's dealership and stores it on the new lead.

**Missing:** Nothing the statement requires. The form itself is the Leads Add lead drawer, not a second form inside the CRM drawer. The customer stays changeable in that drawer: editing the search text clears `customerId` until a customer is picked again. The statement does not say the customer must be locked.

## CR-044

**Statement:** The customer menu should allow notes about customers.

**Status:** implemented

**Place named:** The inventory does not name a route or a field list. The source sentence is only "a way to add notes about customers in the customer menu." W17 places it on the same CRM customer drawer. `05-data-model.md` §4 says the document does not choose one text field versus a note history. `02-new-capabilities.md` §4.4 reads "notes about customers" as its own feature, not the existing lead note.

**Present:**

- `CrmPage` `CustomerForm` includes `notes`. The customer drawer, on both add and edit, has a textarea `id="customer-notes"` labeled "Notes", `maxLength={2000}`, bound to `form.notes`.
- `validateCustomer` rejects notes longer than 2000 characters. `trimForm` trims notes before save. `persist` sends the trimmed form through `customersApi.create` or `customersApi.update` (`frontend/react/src/api/customers.ts`), which post or patch `/api/v1/customers` with optional `notes`.
- `openEdit` loads `r.data.notes` from `customersApi.get` into the textarea.
- `CustomerEntity.Notes` (`backend/dotnet/DealerOps.Core/Customer/CustomerEntity.cs`) maps to `customer.notes`, max length 2000. `CreateCustomerRequest.Notes` and `PatchCustomerRequest.Notes` allow up to 2000 characters and are optional.
- `CustomerService.Create` stores `NormalizeNotes(body.Notes)`. `CustomerService.Patch` writes the same normalized value and, when it changes, audits the `notes` field (new value and previous value). `NormalizeNotes` trims and stores blank notes as null. `ToDetail` returns `customer.Notes` on `CustomerDetail`.
- Startup schema patch in `backend/dotnet/DealerOps.Core/Program.cs` runs `ALTER TABLE customer ADD COLUMN notes VARCHAR(2000) NULL` and ignores a duplicate column.
- This is not `LeadNoteEntity` (`backend/dotnet/DealerOps.Core/Lead/LeadNoteEntity.cs`) and not `POST /api/v1/leads/{id}/notes`. No customer-note entity or note-list route was found under `backend/dotnet/DealerOps.Core/Customer`.

**Missing:** Nothing the statement requires. Notes are one optional text field on the customer, replaced on the next save, not an append-only list with author and time. The CRM table does not show the notes. The statement does not require those.
