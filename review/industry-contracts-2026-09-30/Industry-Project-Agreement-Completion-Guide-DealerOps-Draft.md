## Schedule A draft wording

**1. Scope of Project**  
Build DealerOps, a multi-dealership web platform for used-vehicle dealership operations. The agreed course scope includes DMS vehicle records, CRM customer records and vehicle links, OMVIC-focused advertising checks, an audit history for DMS/CRM changes, and an in-dealership read-only AI assistant. The four independently deployable apps are dealer-web, dealer-gateway, dealer-core and ai-service; dealer-platform holds infrastructure-as-code materials. Browser requests pass through the API Gateway. Sign-in uses Microsoft Entra ID.

**2. Intended Outcome**  
Deliver a working classroom demonstration with two isolated dealerships and staff users, an administrator who cannot access dealership business records, audited DMS/CRM changes, a real AI ad-check call, and a read-only assistant. Sprint 2 and Sprint 3 demonstrations run in Azure over HTTPS through the cloud Gateway.

**3. Schedule of Student Activities**  
Work as a four-person Scrum team using the shared Scrum board. Plan work, hold class syncs and retrospectives, record client decisions and action items, and submit each student’s progress reports as required by the course. Each student presents their own evidence at each of the three Sprint Reviews. Deliver and demonstrate the four apps and their separate pipelines.

**4. Milestones and Timetable**  
- Sprint 1 / Review 1: independently build the four apps; show their pipelines and architecture; demonstrate Gateway routing and Entra roles. Local demonstration is allowed for this review.
- Sprint 2 / Review 2: demonstrate the cloud HTTPS path from Entra sign-in through Gateway to business APIs and a real AI ad check; show Docker, Azure, Bicep, CI/CD, RBAC and Key Vault practices.
- Sprint 3 / Review 3: demonstrate the full cloud flow, including dealership isolation, CRM links, CASH/FINANCE/LEASE ad checks, TXT export, DMS/CRM audit history and the read-only assistant.
- Final showcase: present the completed project in the course’s Week 14 or 15 showcase window.

These are course week references, not calendar dates. Add exact dates only after the assigned instructor confirms them.

**5. SAIT Instructor and Organization Mentor**  
Enter the assigned instructor and any SAIT-approved Organization Mentor after written confirmation. Neither is established as the Organization’s representative by the files reviewed.
